## 2. Content & Censorship Variations
- **Sega CD (North America / Europe):** Altered character ages (Katrina Gibson aged to 18), modified strip club sequence, recolored blood, and censorship of exposed anatomy.
- **PlayStation & Saturn:** Increased mosaic filters on gore/violence compared to PC Engine, but feature full resolution 320x240 CG cutscenes.
- **PC Engine Super CD-ROM2:** Definitive 16-color Japanese uncut release that established the canonical Act 3 scenario script.
